# Capture and measurement workspace

- `cad/`: optional camera stands, object holders, or measurement fixtures.
- `prints/`: small intentional fixture exports with source links and print settings.

Start with opaque, rigid, mostly convex objects. Capture sharp views from different angles under consistent lighting. Record object dimensions in metres and mass in kilograms when measured; document the measuring method. Label unavailable properties rather than inventing measurements.

Use the wrist camera, external camera, or a phone based on image quality. The SO-101 may support a future automated scanning experiment; arm motion and assembly printing are not required for the core software demo. Keep existing camera and robot work available in its branches and history.

Store large captures under ignored `data/` or `artifacts/` and share explicit artifact locations in team notes. No new hardware purchase is required by this plan.
